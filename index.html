Curried Sweet Potato, Apple & Black Bean Quinoa Bowl (Vegan)

Hearty, healthy, and made with pantry staples

Description

This cozy vegan bowl brings together sweet roasted apples and sweet potatoes, savory black beans, fluffy tri-color quinoa, and a creamy dollop of coconut yogurt. Delicious, easy, and ready in under 45 minutes!

Ingredients

  • 1 large sweet potato, peeled & diced
  • 1 apple, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 cup tri-color quinoa
  • 1 can (or 1½ cups) black beans
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil or water
  • 2 tbsp coconut yogurt
  • 1–2 tortillas

Instructions

  1. Cook quinoa: Simmer 1 cup quinoa with 2 cups water for ~15 mins until fluffy.
  2. Roast sweet potatoes & apples with curry powder at 400°F for 25–30 mins.
  3. Sauté onions, add black beans and warm through.
  4. Assemble: Layer quinoa, sweet potatoes, apples, beans, and top with coconut yogurt.
  5. Serve with warm tortilla.
